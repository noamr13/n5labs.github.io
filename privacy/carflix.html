Privacy Policy — Car Play TV

Effective date: 9 October 2026 (version 1.0) · Developer: N5 Labs · Contact: support@n5labs.online

Short version: Car Play TV keeps your library on your device. It has no advertising and no usage analytics. A subscription service handles purchases. The websites, playlists and providers you open receive your requests directly.

1. Who we are

N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent iOS developer. Car Play TV is an app for playing your own media: websites in a built-in browser, your own IPTV playlists and provider accounts, and videos you import.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Your library: source names, media titles, groups, favorites, playback progress and TV guide data, in the app's local storage.
  • Stream addresses and provider logins, in the device Keychain.
  • Videos you import from Files, copied into the app for offline playback.
  • Browser bookmarks and pages you choose to save for next launch (titles and addresses only), in the Keychain. Cookies, website sign-ins and browsing history belong to temporary sessions and are not kept for the next launch.
  • A counter of the free minutes you have used, in the Keychain. It holds a number only and can remain after the app is deleted.

None of this is sent to us. We do not run a server that receives your library, your sources, what you watch or what you browse.

3. Connections you choose

Importing an online playlist or TV guide, connecting a provider account, playing a remote stream and opening a website send requests from your device to that provider or website. They receive your network address and any account details you enter. Their own privacy policies apply, and websites may collect data or show advertising.

4. Subscription

Car Play TV Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage the subscription. RevenueCat receives your purchase status and history for this app, an anonymous app user ID, and basic technical information such as app version, device type and country. If you installed the app from an Apple Ads advert, RevenueCat also receives Apple's attribution token so that we can tell which advert led to a subscription. This is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising in the app and no advertising identifiers.
  • No usage analytics: we do not record what you watch, browse or add.
  • No sale of personal data.

6. Your choices

  • In Sources you can remove a source (its media, favorites and saved login), clear playback history and clear the TV guide.
  • In the browser you can clear a tab or clear all browser data, including bookmarks and saved pages.
  • Deleting the app removes its local storage. To ask us to delete the subscription record held by RevenueCat, write to the address above.

7. Children

Car Play TV is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
