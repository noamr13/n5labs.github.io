Kalo Dromo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Publisher: N5 Labs.

What the app stores

Your answers, mistakes, bookmarks, mock-test results, language, licence and test date are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them; Settings → Reset progress clears them.

Purchases

Kalo Dromo Pro is sold through the App Store. To check whether Pro is active we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your App Store purchase receipts and basic device information (such as the iOS version and country). RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. If you arrived from an Apple Search Ads advert, Apple's attribution token is shared with RevenueCat to measure the campaign; it does not identify you.

What we do not do

No account, no advertising, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, no location, no contacts, no analytics SDK.

Children

The app is meant for people preparing for a driving licence and is not directed at children under 13.

Οι απαντήσεις, τα λάθη, τα διαγωνίσματα και οι ρυθμίσεις σου μένουν μόνο στη συσκευή σου.
