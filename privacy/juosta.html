Privacy Policy — Juosta

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Developer: N5 Labs · Contact: support@n5labs.online
Short version: Juosta helps you prepare for the Lithuanian driving theory exam (KET, category B). Your progress, answers, streak, goal, daily reminder, exam date and language choices stay on your device. There is no account and no login. We do not sell or share personal data, and the App contains no advertising.

1. Who we are

N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent iOS developer. Juosta (the "App") is an iPhone app with practice questions, timed practice exams, mistake reviews and a road-term glossary for the Lithuanian theory exam, readable in Lithuanian, English, Ukrainian or Russian.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Your answers, scores, practice and exam history, mistake reviews and glossary progress.
  • The exam date you enter (optional) and your study and exam language.
  • Your study goal, your daily pace (minutes per day), your daily streak and the stats built from your answers (answers per day, correct answers per block, exams taken and passed), and the first name you may enter (optional).
  • Your daily reminder settings (on or off, and the time). The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone after you allow notifications; it is not sent from a server, and the App works without it.

This data is stored locally using Apple's standard storage. It is never uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see it. Deleting the App deletes it. You can also clear your study progress at any time with Reset progress in the App's profile screen.

3. Data the App does not collect

  • We receive no name, email, phone number, address or government identifier (a first name you type into the App stays on your device). No account or sign-up.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. The minimal data we do use

  • Anonymous subscription identifier. RevenueCat assigns the App a random ID that is not derived from any personal information. It is used only to check whether you have Juosta Pro.
  • Purchase records. When you buy or restore Juosta Pro, Apple and RevenueCat process the transaction. Apple handles all payment details; we never see card or payment information. We also use these records, in aggregate, to measure how many people subscribe and which ad campaigns lead to purchases.
  • Apple Search Ads attribution. If you installed the App from an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple's AdServices framework provides an anonymous attribution token that is sent to RevenueCat so we can see which ad campaign led to an install or purchase. It does not identify you and is not used to track you across apps.

5. Subscriptions

Juosta Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the Apple App Store and managed with RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store account settings.

6. Third-party services

  • RevenueCat: subscription status and purchase records, using the anonymous ID. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: distribution, payments and Search Ads attribution. apple.com/legal/privacy

7. Retention

Study data lives on your device until you delete the App. RevenueCat keeps anonymous purchase records for as long as needed to provide and verify your subscription and to meet legal obligations.

8. Children

Juosta is meant for people preparing for a driving licence and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

If you are in the EU (including Lithuania) or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because the App has no account and keeps your study data only on your device, most of it is already under your direct control. For anything else, email support@n5labs.online. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Lithuania, the Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija).

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@n5labs.online.
