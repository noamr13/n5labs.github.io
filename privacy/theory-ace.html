Privacy Policy

Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026 · Last updated 24 September 2026

Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026 works without an account and without sign-up. This policy explains what data is processed even so.

Who this is

Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026 is published by N5 Labs, reachable at support@n5labs.online.

What stays on your device

Your practice progress, streaks, saved questions, test date and test centre, learner permit expiry date, and any EDT (Essential Driver Training) lessons you type in are stored only on your device. This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party; we have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by iOS on your device. There is no push server and no data leaves your device to make the reminder work.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics SDK. The only detail about your use of the app that leaves your device is which screen opened the Pro screen (see below).
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No data is sold or shared for marketing.
  • No third-party SDKs, other than RevenueCat for the optional subscription (see below).

Subscription and payment

The app offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, Theory Ace Pro. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

To manage subscription status we use RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID, randomly generated on your device. This ID is not linked to your name or email, because we don't have that information;
  • the purchase/transaction information and your subscription status;
  • Apple's identifier for vendor, a device identifier that is not the advertising identifier;
  • basic device and app information: the app name, app and build version, the language the app runs in, iOS version, device model, preferred languages and App Store country;
  • which screen opened the Pro screen: the offer shown once after you set up the app, a Pro-only practice mode, or the results screen at the end of a session;
  • the Apple Ads attribution token described below;
  • the IP address your connection comes from, as with any request over the internet.

RevenueCat uses this to validate the subscription and to handle purchases and restores. We also use it to see which parts of the app lead learners to subscribe. RevenueCat acts as our data processor and does not use this data to advertise to you.

You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Apple Ads

Once per installation, the app asks Apple's AdServices framework for an attribution token and sends it to RevenueCat. RevenueCat uses the token to check with Apple whether the install came from one of our Apple Ads campaigns, so we can measure how those campaigns perform. The attribution data is what Apple provides. The token is not the advertising identifier, the app does not ask to track you, and none of this is used to follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data on our own servers, there is normally nothing for us to show, correct, or delete. For the pseudonymous record RevenueCat holds for us (the anonymous ID and the details listed above), you can contact us by email and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it. Include the order ID from your Apple receipt so we can find the right record; without a purchase, we have no way to tell which record is yours. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, including the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Children

Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026 is intended for learner drivers preparing for the Irish category B (car) driver theory test — in Ireland, category B learner permits are available from age 17, and the app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independence

Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026 is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Road Safety Authority (RSA) or the Driver Theory Testing Service. The practice questions in the app are original questions written by us, not the official test bank. Road sign diagrams shown in the app are public-domain Irish road sign artwork.
