Privacy Policy

ARDMS & CCI sonography exam-prep app · Effective date: 11 September 2026 · Developer: N5 Labs · Contact: support@n5labs.com
Short version: This is a study app for sonography registry exams. There is no account and no login. Your study progress lives on your device. We use RevenueCat to manage the optional subscription and PostHog for anonymous product analytics so we can improve the app. We show no ads, we do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not sell or share your personal data or track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who we are

N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent iOS developer. This app helps students and sonographers prepare for ARDMS and CCI credentialing exams with practice questions, explanations, flashcards, and physics tools. There is no user account and no login.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your study progress — answered questions, scores, streaks, saved flashcards, and settings — is stored locally on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see it.
  • The question bank and images are bundled in the app; browsing or answering them happens entirely on your device.

3. Subscriptions

The app offers an optional subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple; we use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and validate receipts. We never see your card or Apple ID. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, app-specific identifier and your subscription status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

4. Analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used — for example which screens are opened and which study modes are used — so we can improve it. These events are tied to a random, app-generated identifier, not to your name or Apple ID. This data is product analytics only. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you across other apps or websites. See PostHog's privacy policy.

5. What we collect, in App Store terms

  • Purchases — your subscription status (via RevenueCat), not linked to your identity.
  • Product interaction & app performance — anonymous usage and diagnostic events (via PostHog), not linked to your identity.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, health records, or browsing history.

6. Children

This app is intended for adult students and healthcare professionals preparing for professional credentialing exams. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Third parties we rely on

  • Apple — App Store purchases (StoreKit).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics.

8. Your rights

Because the app has no account and stores your study data on your device, you can remove everything by resetting progress in Settings or deleting the app. For questions or requests, email support@n5labs.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
