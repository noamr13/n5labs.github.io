Privacy Policy — Chess Coach

Effective date: 29 June 2026 · Developer: N5 Labs · Contact: support@n5labs.com
Short version: Chess Coach is a browser extension that runs only on chess.com learning pages. It reads the board locally in your browser to teach you — it collects no personal data, stores nothing, sets no cookies, and sends nothing to any server.

1. Who we are

N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent software developer. Chess Coach is a browser extension that acts as a real-time chess tutor on chess.com learning surfaces — the analysis board, game review, puzzles, and games against the computer.

2. What data we collect

None. Chess Coach does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or user data. It has no backend server and makes no network requests of its own.

3. What the extension reads, and why

To teach, the extension reads the current chess position (the arrangement of pieces) from the chess.com page you are viewing. This happens entirely inside your browser, in memory, so the extension can draw arrows on the board and show or speak short explanations. This board information is never stored and never sent anywhere; it is used momentarily to generate coaching and then discarded as the position changes.

4. Permissions

Chess Coach requests host access to chess.com only (https://www.chess.com/*). This is used solely to read the on-screen board and to draw its coaching overlay on chess.com pages. The extension runs on no other website and requests no other permissions.

5. Voice

Optional spoken coaching uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis (the Web Speech API). No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted by the extension.

6. Fair play

Chess Coach is a learning tool. It operates only on analysis, game review, puzzle, and vs-computer pages, and is designed to stay inactive during live games against other people.

7. No accounts, tracking, or ads

There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no cookies set by the extension.

8. Children

Chess Coach is a general learning utility. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. No sale of personal information

N5 Labs does not sell personal information. The extension does not collect any to begin with.

10. Your choices

You can remove Chess Coach at any time from chrome://extensions. It leaves no data behind. For questions, email support@n5labs.com.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
