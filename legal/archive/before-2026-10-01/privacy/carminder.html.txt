  <h1>Privacy Policy — Car Play Widgets</h1>
  <div class="meta">Effective date: 29 September 2026 (version 1.6) · Developer: N5 Labs · Contact: <a href="mailto:support@n5labs.online">support@n5labs.online</a></div>

  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Short version:</strong> Car Play Widgets stores widget setup on your device, uses Apple Shortcuts for optional ride sounds, and can look up timed lyrics for songs playing in Apple Music. It uses analytics and subscription services and measures its own ads without tracking you.
  </div>

  <h2>1. Who we are</h2>
  <p>N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent iOS developer. Car Play Widgets (formerly CarMinder) is an iPhone app for car dashboard widgets, lyrics, motion-poster cards, and ride sound setup.</p>

  <h2>2. Data stored on your device</h2>
  <p>Car Play Widgets may store these settings locally on your device:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Car nickname, model name, city, dashboard color, and selected widget style.</li>
    <li>Selected car image and widget preview data used by the WidgetKit extension.</li>
    <li>Lyrics-card text, the selected song's title and artist, timed lyric state, theme, and line progress. The latest lyric may be stored in the shared container for the Lyrics widget.</li>
    <li>Motion-poster title, subtitle, frame style, and selected frame.</li>
    <li>Selected ride sounds and imported custom sound files for CarPlay connected, CarPlay disconnected, and home arrival cues.</li>
    <li>Pro entitlement state used to unlock paid features.</li>
    <li>An anonymous analytics install identifier when analytics is configured, and the Airbridge device ID used for ad measurement (section 7).</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>3. Widgets and photos</h2>
  <p>When you choose a car image, Car Play Widgets processes it on device into a bounded widget image and stores it in the app's shared container so the WidgetKit extension can display it. The app does not request broad photo-library permission for this feature.</p>

  <h2>3.1 AI Widgets (optional feature)</h2>
  <p>If you use the optional AI Widgets feature, the car photo and the person photo you explicitly select are sent over an encrypted connection to our secure relay (hosted on Cloudflare) and passed to our image-generation provider, fal.ai, solely to create your personalized widget image. The photos are used only for that single generation: they are not used to train AI models, are not used for advertising or marketing, and are not shared with anyone else. The generated widget image is returned to your device and stored locally. AI generations use credits, which can be included with a Pro subscription or purchased through Apple's in-app purchase system; we never see your payment details. If you never use AI Widgets, no photos leave your device.</p>

  <h2>3.2 Apple Music and timed lyrics</h2>
  <p>If you connect Apple Music and separately allow lyric lookups, the app reads the current song and playback position. It sends the song title, artist, album when available, and duration to <strong>LRCLIB</strong> over HTTPS to find timed lyrics. LRCLIB also receives your network IP address as part of the request. The app does not send your Apple account credentials or song audio to LRCLIB. You can turn off lyric lookups in the app; this stops new requests. Lyric and widget state may be stored locally to display the current line. See <a href="https://lrclib.net/">LRCLIB</a> for its service information.</p>

  <h2>4. Notifications and Shortcuts</h2>
  <p>Car Play Widgets can provide local notification sounds for Apple Shortcuts automations. Notification permission may be requested when you enable or run sound cues. Imported custom sounds stay on your device and are used only for local sound cues. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.</p>
  <p>Apple Shortcuts runs personal automations such as CarPlay connect, CarPlay disconnect, and Arrive. Car Play Widgets provides App Shortcuts plus bundled or user-imported local sound cues; it does not control the CarPlay interface.</p>

  <h2>5. Location</h2>
  <p>Car Play Widgets does not request GPS location permission. Home arrival automations are configured and run by Apple's Shortcuts app. Our analytics or measurement providers may estimate an approximate region, such as your country or city, from your IP address; this is not precise GPS location.</p>

  <h2>6. Purchases</h2>
  <p>Car Play Widgets may offer optional Pro features through auto-renewable subscriptions sold by Apple through your App Store account. We use <strong>RevenueCat</strong> to manage subscription entitlements and validate purchase status. RevenueCat processes purchase identifiers and subscription history needed to unlock Pro features. We never see your payment card or Apple ID password. RevenueCat also sends subscription lifecycle events and purchase values to Airbridge for ad measurement as described below.</p>
  <p>See <a href="https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy">RevenueCat's privacy policy</a> and <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/">Apple's privacy policy</a>.</p>

  <h2>7. Analytics and ad measurement</h2>
  <p>Car Play Widgets sends product analytics events through <strong>Google Firebase Analytics</strong>, and through <strong>PostHog</strong> when an analytics key is configured. Events are designed to avoid personal content, precise location, license plates, phone numbers, names, messages, and audio URLs.</p>
  <p>We use Firebase Analytics, Apple Ads attribution, and Apple's SKAdNetwork to measure installs and purchases that come from our own ads. RevenueCat may send purchase events to Firebase for this purpose. SKAdNetwork measurement is aggregated by Apple.</p>
  <p>Car Play Widgets includes the restricted version of the SDK from <strong>Airbridge</strong>, our measurement partner, so we can see which of our own ads lead to installs and subscriptions. It collects Apple's app-specific identifier for vendors (IDFV) and an Airbridge device ID; basic device and app information, such as device model, OS and app version, language, and a country estimated from your IP address; app events such as installing, opening the app, and finishing onboarding; and, when an install came from an ad, Apple Ads attribution data and Google's on-device conversion measurement data. RevenueCat also sends subscription events, such as a new subscription or a renewal with its price and currency, from its servers to Airbridge together with the Airbridge device ID, so a subscription can be matched to the install it came from. Airbridge's restricted SDK does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we use this data only to measure our own ads.</p>
  <p>Car Play Widgets does not ask for tracking permission, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Versions 1.2 and 1.3 included Meta's SDK and asked for tracking permission; version 1.4 removed both.</p>
  <p>See <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google's privacy policy</a>, <a href="https://posthog.com/privacy">PostHog's privacy policy</a>, and <a href="https://www.airbridge.io/en/privacy-policy">Airbridge's privacy policy</a>.</p>

  <h2>8. Children</h2>
  <p>Car Play Widgets is a general lifestyle utility. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.</p>

  <h2>9. No sale of personal information</h2>
  <p>N5 Labs does not sell personal information.</p>

  <h2>10. Your choices</h2>
  <p>You can remove locally stored Car Play Widgets data by deleting the app from your device. You can turn off lyric lookups in the app. For questions or requests, email <a href="mailto:support@n5labs.online">support@n5labs.online</a>.</p>

  <h2>11. Changes</h2>
  <p>We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.</p>
