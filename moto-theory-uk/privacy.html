Privacy Policy

Moto Theory · Last updated 15 September 2026

Moto Theory never asks for your name, email or anything else that identifies you. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. This policy explains the little data that does exist and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Your study progress is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own local storage. That includes:

  • the questions you have answered, how often you got each one right, and your mistakes list;
  • your mock test results, including the answers you chose and the time you took;
  • your study plan: whether your test is booked, your test date if you enter one (or an estimate a month ahead if you pick that option), your licence path (CBT, A1 or A2) and your daily goal;
  • your progress counters, including your miles and the days you studied (for your streak), and your sound and haptics settings.

None of this is sent to us, and we have no way to read it. The app does not ask for your name or email address. Your progress is not synced to iCloud, although it is included in your normal iPhone backups like other app data.

You can wipe it at any time with Reset all progress on the You screen, which deletes your progress from the phone.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access. The app asks for no device permissions at all, and it does not send you notifications.
  • No advertising identifier. The app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting on how you use the app.

Sound effects are generated on the device. The app does not record audio.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Moto Theory Pro, payment is handled by Apple through your Apple Account. The app itself never asks for or handles your card details.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat is the only online service the app connects to. It checks your status when the app starts, when the app comes back to the foreground and when you open the Pro screen, whether or not you have subscribed, and it handles purchases and restores.

RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID, randomly generated on your device, which is not linked to your name or email because we do not have them;
  • your purchase and subscription status, including the purchase transactions and receipts Apple issues;
  • Apple's identifier for vendor, a device identifier that is not the advertising identifier;
  • basic technical details such as the app and build version, iOS version, device model, preferred languages, App Store country, and whether a purchase is a test purchase;
  • the IP address your connection comes from, as with any request over the internet.

RevenueCat keeps the anonymous ID and a cached copy of your subscription status on your device. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how it handles this data.

Links

Links in the app to this policy and to Apple's standard licence agreement open in your web browser. The app does not load them itself.

Children

Moto Theory is aimed at people preparing for the UK motorcycle theory test. The app asks no one, children included, for their name, contact details or age, and the only data that leaves the phone is the subscription check described above.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over personal data about you. We keep none on our own systems. The only record outside your phone is the subscription record RevenueCat holds for us, described above. It is not linked to your name or email. To have it removed, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it. Include the order ID from your Apple receipt so we can find the right record; without a purchase, we have no way to tell which record is yours.
