Restore a purchase

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which exams does it cover?

Practice content spans the ARDMS SPI (ultrasound physics) exam and the ARDMS specialty exams (Abdomen, OB/GYN, Vascular, Adult Echo, Breast, Pediatric Sonography, Fetal Echo, Pediatric Echo, Musculoskeletal), plus CCI cardiac content.

Is this official?

No. This is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Inteleos, ARDMS, or CCI. All questions are original practice items written against the public exam content outlines. They are not real, recalled, or retired exam questions, and we do not guarantee any exam result. Always confirm current exam requirements with the official credentialing body.

Report a content issue
