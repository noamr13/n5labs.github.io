What is Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026?

An iPhone practice app for the Irish category B (car) driver theory test. It has no account and no sign-up: your progress, streaks, saved questions, test date and centre, learner permit expiry date, and any EDT lessons you type in are stored only on your device.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the paywall in the app and tap Restore. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and cancel Theory Ace Pro there. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I reset my stats?

In the app, use Reset my stats to clear your practice progress. Deleting the app also removes all locally stored data, since nothing is backed up to a server.

Are these the official test questions?

No. The practice questions are original questions written by us, not the official test bank. Theory Ace: DTT Ireland 2026 is an independent app: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Road Safety Authority (RSA) or the Driver Theory Testing Service. Road sign diagrams shown in the app are public-domain Irish road sign artwork.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when the app is deleted, or when you use Reset my stats. There is no account, so progress can't be recovered after a device change.
