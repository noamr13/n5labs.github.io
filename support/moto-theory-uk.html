Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login, and everything except purchases works offline.

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also start again with Reset all progress on the You tab.

How does the mock exam work?
50 questions in 57 minutes, and you need 43 right to pass, the same as the real multiple-choice test. You can flag questions and go back before you finish. An unanswered question counts as wrong.

When does a question leave my Mistakes list?
After you answer it correctly twice.

What is free and what is Pro?
Free: the first three legs of the route, one full mock exam, every explanation, your mistakes list and your readiness report. Pro unlocks all nine legs and unlimited mock exams, as a weekly or monthly subscription.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?
Cancel in your iPhone's Settings, under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews. To restore a purchase on a new phone, tap Restore on the Pro screen or the You tab.

Is hazard perception included?
Not in this version. The app covers the multiple-choice part of the test.

I think an answer is wrong.
Email us with the question text and we will check it.
