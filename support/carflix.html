Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Car Play TV has no sign-up. Your library stays on your device.

Does the app come with channels or shows?
No. It is a player and a browser. You add your own playlists, provider accounts, videos and website sign-ins, and you need the right to watch what you add.

How do I add a playlist?
Open Sources and add an M3U or M3U8 link, pick a playlist file, scan a QR code, or sign in to an Xtream account. A TV guide (XMLTV) can be added the same way.

Nothing shows on my car display.
Video on the car display uses Apple CarPlay. It needs a car and an iOS version that allow video there, and it works only while the car is parked. If your car does not allow it, the app still works on your iPhone and iPad.

A website opens but the video stays black.
Some services protect their video and do not play in a browser. That is decided by the service, not by the app.

How many free minutes do I get?
30 minutes of playback and browsing in total. Car Play TV Pro removes the limit.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?
Open Sources to restore purchases or to manage your subscription. You can also cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?
Remove a source in Sources to remove its media, favorites and saved login. The browser's actions menu clears tabs, bookmarks and saved pages. Deleting the app removes the library.
