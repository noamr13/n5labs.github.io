Short version: Flagfall helps you prepare for the Hong Kong Transport Department taxi and ride-hailing written test. Your progress, answers, mock results and exam date stay on your device. There is no account, no login and no advertising. We do not sell or share personal data.

1. Who we are

N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent iOS developer. 起錶 Flagfall (the "App") is an iPhone and iPad app with practice questions, location and route drills, timed mock exams and mistake reviews for the Taxi and Ride-hailing Vehicle Combined Written Test.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Your answers, scores, practice and mock history, mistake reviews, streak and daily goal.
  • The exam date and study preferences you choose during setup (optional).

This data is stored locally using Apple's standard storage. It is never uploaded to our servers and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. Nothing about the reminder is sent to us. You can turn it off in the App (我的) or in iOS Settings.

4. Data the App does not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, address, HKID or other government identifier. No account or sign-up.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. The minimal data we do use

  • Anonymous purchase identifier. RevenueCat assigns the App a random ID that is not derived from any personal information. It is used only to check whether you have 起錶 Pro.
  • Purchase records. When you buy or restore 起錶 Pro, Apple and RevenueCat process the transaction. Apple handles all payment details; we never see card or payment information.
  • Apple Search Ads attribution. If you installed the App from an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple's AdServices framework provides an anonymous attribution token that is sent to RevenueCat so we can see which ad campaign led to an install or purchase. It does not identify you and is not used to track you across apps.

6. Purchases

起錶 Pro is offered as a one-time lifetime purchase or as a weekly auto-renewing subscription, sold through the Apple App Store and managed with RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

7. Third-party services

  • RevenueCat: purchase status and records, using the anonymous ID. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: distribution, payments and Search Ads attribution. apple.com/legal/privacy

8. Retention

Study data lives on your device until you delete the App. RevenueCat keeps anonymous purchase records for as long as needed to provide and verify your purchase and to meet legal obligations.

9. Children

The App is meant for adults preparing for a driving-related licence test and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

Under the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, and the GDPR where it applies, you may request access to or correction of personal data we hold. Because the App has no account and keeps your study data only on your device, most of it is already under your direct control. For anything else, email support@n5labs.online.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@n5labs.online.
