恢復購買

  • 打開起錶，去 我的，㩒 恢復購買。請用購買起錶 Pro 時嘅同一個 Apple 帳戶。
  • Pro 頁面底部都有 恢復購買。

取消每週訂閱

  • 打開 iPhone 嘅「設定」，㩒你嘅名，再㩒 訂閱項目。
  • 揀起錶，㩒 取消訂閱。已付費嘅期間完結前，Pro 會繼續有效。
  • 退款由 Apple 處理：reportaproblem.apple.com。

Contact

Restore purchases

Open the app, go to 我的 (Me) and tap 恢復購買 (Restore purchases), signed in with the Apple Account you bought 起錶 Pro with. It is also at the bottom of the Pro screen.

Cancel the weekly subscription

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose 起錶 and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your progress

Your progress stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
