Privacy Policy

CSCS Prep · Last updated 12 September 2026

CSCS Prep does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, and no login. This policy explains the little that does happen.

What stays on your device

Your revision progress — which questions you have answered, what you got wrong, your mock test scores and your streak — is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us, and we have no way to read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar or health data.
  • No advertising identifier. The app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics on how you use the app.

Purchases

If you buy CSCS Prep Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details or your Apple Account.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated identifier for your installation, plus the purchase receipt Apple issues. That identifier is not linked to your name or email, because we do not have them. RevenueCat acts as our data processor and does not use this to advertise to you.

Children

CSCS Prep is aimed at people preparing for a UK construction industry test and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over personal data we hold about you. In practice we hold none, so there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. If you want the pseudonymous purchase record removed, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date at the top.
