Privacy Policy

CSCS Prep · Last updated 24 September 2026

CSCS Prep never asks for your name, email or anything else that identifies you. There is no account, no sign-up, and no login. This policy explains the little that does happen.

What stays on your device

Your revision progress — which questions you have answered, what you got wrong, your mock test scores and your streak — is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us, and we have no way to read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar or health data.
  • No advertising identifier. The app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics on how you use the app, and no crash reporting. The only detail about your use of the app that leaves the phone is which part of the app opened the Pro screen, described under Purchases.

Purchases

If you buy CSCS Prep Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details or your Apple Account.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated identifier for your installation, which is not linked to your name or email, because we do not have them;
  • your purchase and subscription status, including the purchase receipt Apple issues;
  • Apple's identifier for vendor, a device identifier that is not the advertising identifier;
  • basic technical details such as the app name, app and build version, the language the app runs in, iOS version, device model, preferred languages and App Store country;
  • which part of the app opened the Pro screen: the mock test limit, a specialist topic (with that topic's name) or Settings;
  • the Apple Ads attribution token described below;
  • the IP address your connection comes from, as with any request over the internet.

RevenueCat uses this to check your subscription and to handle purchases and restores. We also use it to see which parts of the app lead people to subscribe. RevenueCat acts as our data processor and does not use this data to advertise to you.

Apple Ads

Once per installation, the app asks Apple's AdServices framework for an attribution token and sends it to RevenueCat. RevenueCat uses the token to check with Apple whether the install came from one of our Apple Ads campaigns, so we can measure how those campaigns perform. The attribution data is what Apple provides. The token is not the advertising identifier, the app does not ask to track you, and none of this is used to follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

CSCS Prep is aimed at people preparing for a UK construction industry test and is not directed at children. The app asks no one, children included, for their name, contact details or age.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over personal data about you. We keep none on our own systems. The only record outside your phone is the pseudonymous one RevenueCat holds for us, described above. To have it removed, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it. Include the order ID from your Apple receipt so we can find the right record; without a purchase, we have no way to tell which record is yours.
