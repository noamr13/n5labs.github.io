3. What Car Play Widgets does

Car Play Widgets provides car dashboard widget customization, text cards with words you type, WidgetKit-safe motion-poster styling, and ride sound setup through Apple Shortcuts. It also provides Live Lyrics with Apple Music, AI Widgets, a GIF Library and AI voice announcements, described in sections 4 and 5.

4. Widgets, lyrics, and motion posters

Widget display and availability are handled by iOS and WidgetKit. Motion-poster cards are static WidgetKit views that can refresh over time; they do not play video inside widgets.

Live Lyrics. When you connect Apple Music in the Lyrics tab, the App reads the title, artist, album, duration and playback position of the song playing in the Music app. After you allow lyric lookups, it sends the title, artist, album and duration to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free community lyrics database run by a third party, and shows the time-synced lyrics it returns. The App does not bundle a lyrics catalog, does not send audio, and you can turn lookups off at any time. Not every song has synced lyrics, and lyrics may be incomplete or inaccurate. Lyrics belong to their respective owners and are shown for your personal use only. With Pro, lyrics can also appear in a Home Screen widget and a Live Activity.

You are responsible for the text you type into text cards.

5. AI features and the GIF Library

  • AI Widgets. You pick a photo of your car and a photo of a person. After you confirm, both photos are sent through our relay to fal.ai, an AI image service, which creates one widget picture of a 3D avatar next to the car. Each picture uses one AI credit.
  • AI voice announcements. A short phrase you type is sent through our relay to ElevenLabs, an AI voice service, which creates a spoken cue. Each voice uses one AI credit. Keeping a custom sound requires Pro.
  • GIF Library. Search and trending results come from GIPHY, and your search words are sent to GIPHY. The widget shows the chosen GIF's still frame, because Home Screen widgets do not animate. GIPHY content belongs to its owners and is subject to GIPHY's terms.
  • Only upload photos you have the right to use, and only photos of people who agreed to it. Do not use these features to create unlawful, harmful, deceptive or infringing content.
  • AI results are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or not what you expected. Third-party services can be unavailable, and we may change or replace them.
  • How this data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

6. Apple Shortcuts and CarPlay

Apple Shortcuts runs personal automations such as CarPlay connect, CarPlay disconnect, and Arrive. Car Play Widgets provides sound cue actions, bundled local sounds, and user-imported local audio for custom cues. Automation behavior can depend on iOS settings, notification permission, Focus modes, Silent Mode, volume, and device state.

On iOS 26 and later, iOS can show Home Screen widgets and the lyrics Live Activity on the CarPlay screen. Car Play Widgets has no CarPlay app of its own and does not control, modify, or replace the CarPlay interface.
