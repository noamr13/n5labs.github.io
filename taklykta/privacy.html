Privacy Policy — Taklykta

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Developer: N5 Labs · Contact: support@n5labs.online
Short version: Taklykta helps you prepare for the Swedish taxi driver licence (taxiförarlegitimation) theory tests. Your progress, answers, points and test dates stay on your device. There is no account and no login. We do not sell or share personal data, and the App contains no advertising.

1. Who we are

N5 Labs ("we", "us") is an independent iOS developer. Taklykta (the "App") is an iPhone app with practice questions, timed mock tests, law references, map exercises, mistake reviews and a six-month window planner for the two Swedish taxi theory tests.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Your answers, scores, practice and exam history, mistake reviews, points and streaks.
  • The test dates you enter (optional).

This data is stored locally using Apple's standard storage. It is never uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see it. Your progress stays on your phone. You can delete it at any time in the App under Inställningar (Settings, the gear on Hem) with "Nollställ min träning", or by uninstalling the App.

2b. Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. Nothing about the reminder is sent to us. You can turn it off in the App (Inställningar, the gear on Hem) or in iOS Settings.

3. Data the App does not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, address or government identifier. No account or sign-up.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. The minimal data we do use

  • Anonymous subscription identifier. RevenueCat assigns the App a random ID that is not derived from any personal information. It is used only to check whether you have Taklykta Pro.
  • Purchase records. When you buy or restore Taklykta Pro, Apple and RevenueCat process the transaction. Apple handles all payment details; we never see card or payment information.
  • Usage context sent to RevenueCat. The App sends RevenueCat the following attributes, and no others. They help us understand which version and which kind of user bought Pro. They never include your answers, scores, test dates or name.
    • tk_app: the name of the App ("taklykta").
    • tk_version: the App version, for example 1.0.
    • tk_build: the App build number.
    • tk_language: your device's preferred language.
    • tk_market: the market the App is made for, always "SE".
    • tk_onboarded: yes or no, whether you finished the first-run setup.
    • tk_test_booked: yes or no, whether you have entered a test date (the date itself is never sent).
    • tk_goal: the study goal you picked during setup.
    • tk_daily_minutes: the minutes a day you chose to study.
    • tk_paywall_source: where you opened the Pro screen from: onboarding, settings, home, drill_limit or mock_limit.
  • Apple Search Ads attribution. If you installed the App from an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple's AdServices framework provides an anonymous attribution token that is sent to RevenueCat so we can see which ad campaign led to an install or purchase. It does not identify you and is not used to track you across apps.

5. Subscriptions

Taklykta Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the Apple App Store and managed with RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store account settings.

6. Third-party services

  • RevenueCat: subscription status and purchase records, using the anonymous ID. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: distribution, payments and Search Ads attribution. apple.com/legal/privacy

7. Retention

Study data lives on your device until you delete it in the App ("Nollställ min träning" under Inställningar) or delete the App. RevenueCat keeps anonymous purchase records for as long as needed to provide and verify your subscription and to meet legal obligations.

8. Children

Taklykta is meant for people preparing for a driving licence and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

If you are in the EU (including Sweden) or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because the App has no account and keeps your study data only on your device, most of it is already under your direct control. For anything else, email support@n5labs.online. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Sweden, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact
